Why the old link stopped working

Every HEIDI dashboard carries the date it was built in its address, and each edition stays put until a newer one replaces it. The edition you were looking for has been retired, which usually means one of two things: the data behind it have been refreshed, or the comparison groups were revised with the institution.

Either way, HEIDI has kept moving since this edition was built, and today's dashboards offer:

  • The newest data from IPEDS, the College Scorecard, the Carnegie Classifications, and other sources.
  • More to compare against: additional measures of finances and financial health, admissions, completions, compensation, and more.
  • A new Portfolio page tracking your degree and certificate program completions over time, and how closely each comparison group's program mix matches yours.
  • Trends in nominal or inflation-adjusted dollars, and a Faculty page that sets your faculty body and average salaries by rank beside each comparison group.
  • A built-in Glossary, with every measure's definition one click from its table or figure.

Send me a note and I will point you to the current edition, or build an updated one if the data have moved on.
