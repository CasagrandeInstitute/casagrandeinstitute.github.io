Why the old link stopped working

Every HEIDI dashboard carries the date it was built in its address, and each edition stays put until a newer one replaces it. The edition you were looking for has been retired, which usually means one of two things: the data behind it have been refreshed, or the comparison groups were revised with the institution.

Either way, the current edition is better than the one you remember. Since this version was built, HEIDI has gained:

  • Fresher IPEDS data, with sample sizes beside every group statistic and the comparability notes that public and for-profit peers call for.
  • A Faculty tab that sets your faculty body and salaries by rank beside the comparison groups, year over year.
  • Cost of Labor, so salary comparisons account for where your comparison institutions actually hire.
  • Group Mode, a dashboard for a whole cohort or association rather than a single institution.

Send me a note and I will point you to the current edition, or build an updated one if the data have moved on.
